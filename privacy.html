EdiBowl helps a household keep track of what is in its kitchen, plan meals and cook from what it has. This page says what the app stores about you, who else receives it, how long it is kept and what you can do about it. We wrote it to be read.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address and a password (stored only as a one-way hash, so we cannot read it), and the name you use in your household.
  • Your household's kitchen: pantry items, the grocery list, recipes, the meal plan, meals you cooked, ratings, notes, and photos of recipes or receipts you add.
  • Allergies, diets and dislikes for each person in the household, including children you add. This is health-related information. You choose what to enter. You can mark an entry private, so other members see only a general warning and never whose it is.
  • Your taste: quiz answers and what the app learns from your ratings.
  • Your settings, such as the theme.
  • A record of AI requests (when, which feature, how much it used and who asked) so we can keep the cost of the AI fair and under control.

On your phone only: your sign-in (in the phone's secure store), a saved copy of your household's data so the app works offline, and the changes you make offline until they can be sent.

Who else receives it

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage. All of the above is stored there.
  • Anthropic provides the AI. Only when you ask for an AI action, it receives what that action needs: for recipe ideas, the names and amounts of pantry items, how soon they expire, allergy and diet words and any personal limits you entered for a diet (never names), recent meal titles, a taste summary and what you typed; for importing, the recipe text or photo you chose; for receipts, the receipt photo; for sorting items into store aisles, the item names. [Confirm and state Anthropic's current terms on retention and training for API data.]
  • Open Food Facts receives the barcode number when you scan a product, to find its name and size.
  • A recipe web page you paste a link to is fetched by our server. No personal data is sent to it.
  • Expo delivers app updates and learns the app version and platform when the app starts.
  • Apple and Google collect what their stores collect when you install the app.

We do not show ads, do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising. We do not use analytics or tracking in the app.

Children

EdiBowl is for adults. An adult household admin can add a child as a profile with no login of their own, and enters the child's name and any allergies or dislikes. The app collects nothing from a child directly, and a child cannot create an account. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your sign-in and everything personal to you is deleted straight away. What you added to a household (pantry items, recipes, grocery requests, meals you cooked) stays with the household without your name; your own ratings and answers go. If you are the last person in a household, the household is deleted with you. Backups, kept in case something goes wrong with our hosting, are encrypted, kept for 35 days (and one a month for a year), and then removed, so data you delete leaves them within that time. [Confirm these periods before launch.] Items you remove from the pantry are kept for 30 days under "Recently removed" so you can bring them back.

Your choices

  • See and take a copy of your data: Settings, then Delete my account, then Get a copy of my data.
  • Correct it: change it in the app.
  • Delete it: Settings, then Delete my account. It takes effect immediately.
  • Ask us: write to edibowlsupport@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live you may have further rights, for example to object or to complain to a data protection authority. [Add the rights and the legal basis that apply, once confirmed.]

Security

Data travels encrypted. Each household can read only its own data, and the database enforces this itself. Sign-in tokens are kept in the phone's secure store. No system is perfectly secure, and the allergy check is an aid, not a guarantee (see the Terms).

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, the app will tell you before it applies. The date at the top is the date of the latest version.
